## Conditions that remain attached to dependencies

- **MIT / BSD / ISC / Zlib / Apache and combined declarations:** keep applicable upstream attribution and legal text with redistributed material. An `OR` permits an offered alternative; an `AND` requires the relevant component terms together. Apache redistribution also preserves applicable notice content. The project does not replace these declarations with its own expression. [Apache redistribution terms](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
- **MPL-2.0 in deployed Wasm:** `wee_alloc 0.4.5` and `memory_units 0.4.0` remain MPL-covered. If shipping compiled Wasm, make their exact corresponding covered source available under MPL, including any modifications, and tell recipients how to obtain it; preserve notices. Original separable BudgetBranch files keep their own terms. [MPL §§3.1–3.4](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/).
- **Geist OFL-1.1:** the font stays OFL, with its actual copyright/license retained in the web distribution. It is unmodified; no font renaming or relicensing occurred. OFL's font conditions do not turn a document or screenshot using the font into OFL font software. [Official OFL terms](https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/).
- **MPL build tools:** Lightning CSS and its platform packages remain MPL-2.0. They are build tools, not the application's browser library. Redistributing their source or native binaries requires their applicable terms, distinct from merely using them in a build. [Mozilla distribution FAQ](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/FAQ/).

The exact MPL source archives are linked in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Their locked SHA-256 values are `dbb3b5a6b2bb17cb6ad44a2e68a43e8d2722c997da10e928665c72ec6c0a0b8e` for wee_alloc and `8452105ba047068f40ff7093dd1d9da90898e63dd61736462e9cdda6a90ad3c3` for memory_units. ## Upstream ambiguities and scope limits

**near-sandbox0.4.1 launcher:** its exact published revision declares MIT OR Apache-2.0 and includes those texts, but also has a root GPLv3 `LICENSE` without a clear application statement found by the targeted review. The archive and exact upstream revision agree. All three texts are retained in the [evidence](evidence/m3/licensing/notices); the detailed review records their provenance. Do not resolve this ambiguity by assuming our license overrides it. Resolve scope before distributing a bundled launcher or host binary. This dependency is outside the normal Wasm graph. [Exact upstream revision](https://github.com/near/near-sandbox-rs/tree/90415de795c3d4706847115c9ebd94afce695d09).

**Other host notices:** security-framework's `THIRD_PARTY` includes APSL-2.0 for documentation adapted from Apple's framework; cfg_aliases has a separate MIT attribution; ring/AWS-LC have mixed component notices; unicode-ident has an additional Unicode grant; certificate-data packages use CDLA-Permissive-2.0. These conditions are recorded separately, not reduced to a blanket MIT/Apache label. The platform-only r-efi declaration offers a permissive alternative to LGPL. No required GPL/LGPL branch was found in package declarations, which does not erase the conflicting near-sandbox text or file-specific notices.

**Missing packaged texts:**29 Cargo packages omit a standalone named license text under the inventory's detection rules, although all declare a license. All eight such packages in the36-crate normal Wasm graph were resolved to exact upstream legal texts and retained. Remaining host/platform omissions and evidence limits are explicit in the inventory. Source-header sampling is not a full copyright audit of every upstream file.

**Prebuilt tools:** native NEAR Sandbox2.14.0 comes from the official artifact host, with verified observed archive/binary pins and a matching nearcore release revision. Its source workspace declares MIT OR Apache-2.0. This does not identify every linked component. Rust's sysroot, Node/Rust distributions and the complete native-tool SBOM are outside the Cargo/npm package inventory. Tools, caches, `node_modules`, native executables and Wasm binaries are not tracked in this Git source distribution; they remain separate upstream downloads. A future binary/tool bundle needs its own complete notice/source-availability review.
